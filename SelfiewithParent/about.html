A thank-you, one selfie at a time

Selfie with Parent is a people's campaign that turns a simple photo into a moment of gratitude, and that gratitude into care for the elders around us.

Who we are

We help families say what often goes unsaid.

Most of us thank our parents far less often than we mean to. Selfie with Parent makes it easy: take a photo together, add one line about what they taught you, and share it.

Every selfie becomes a small, public promise to stay close, and a reminder to look out for elders who have no one to take a picture with.

  • Free for every family
  • Available in six languages
  • Parents, grandparents and guardians all count
  • Your photos, your choice — always with consent

Our mission

Make thanking our parents and grandparents a habit, not a once-a-year message.

Our vision

A world where no elder feels forgotten, whether they live with family, alone, or far away.

Our promise to you

Every photo is shared only with the consent of the people in it, kept safe, and taken down whenever you ask.

What we stand for

Four values behind every selfie

Gratitude first

We celebrate the people who raised us, not likes or followers.

Consent and dignity

No one appears on our site without agreeing to it, least of all an elder.

Every family counts

Single parents, adoptive parents, grandparents raising grandchildren — every family is welcome.

Small acts, often

A weekly call does more than a grand gesture once a year.

How it works

Three steps, two minutes

Take a selfie together

At home, on a video call, or with an old photo if they are no longer with you.

Add one line of thanks

Tell the world one thing they taught you, in any of our six languages.

Share it and keep the promise

Post it on our wall, send it to family, and pick one small promise for the week.

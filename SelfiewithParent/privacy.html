1. Who we are

Selfie with Parent ("we", "us") is run by [organisation name], [registered address]. We decide how your information is used, so we are responsible for it.

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, your parent's or family's name, relationship, city, state and country, mobile number and (optionally) email.
  • What you share: photos, videos, voice notes and messages you upload.
  • For under-18s: your parent or guardian's name and mobile number, so they can confirm consent.
  • Volunteer and school forms: the details you type in those forms.
  • Basic technical data: such as your browser type and language choice, to make the site work. [List any analytics tools here, or state that none are used.]

3. Why we use it

We use your information only with your consent, and only to:

  • review your submission and, if approved, show it in the gallery;
  • contact you about your submission, competitions or the volunteer programme;
  • send reminders you have asked for;
  • keep the site safe and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Children under 18

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before we use your information. We send them a one-time code to confirm. We do not track children's behaviour or show them targeted advertising. A parent or guardian can ask us to remove a child's submission at any time.

5. What is shown publicly

If your submission is approved, we show the photo, video or voice note, the names you entered, your message and your city. Your mobile number, email and your guardian's details are never shown publicly.

6. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the site — for example, hosting and storage, and SMS or WhatsApp messages — under contracts that require them to protect it: [list providers]. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep gallery submissions while the campaign runs, and your contact details for [period]. If you withdraw consent or ask us to delete your information, we remove it within [number] days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Your rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update it.
  • Withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it — as easily as you gave it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.
  • Raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

9. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable security measures, such as encrypted connections and restricted access for our team. If a breach affects your information, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

What we've learned

Three lessons from the journey

Small asks work

People rarely say no to a two-minute photo, and that photo often leads to much more.

Elders want to be heard

A visit means most when we listen to their stories, not just take their picture.

Families come in every shape

Grandparents, guardians and adoptive parents deserve the same thank-you.

The road ahead

Our goals for the next twelve months

Reach more families

Bring the campaign to new states, towns and schools.

More visits to elders

Grow Adopt a Grandparent so more elders who live alone get a weekly visit.

Help on every phone

Run more digital help sessions so parents can stay in touch with family far away.

Be part of the next chapter

Every selfie, every visit and every kept promise is a step on this journey.

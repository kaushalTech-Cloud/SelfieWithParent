Our programmes

Four ways we care for elders

Elder health

Free check-up camps and medicine support for senior citizens in rural areas.

  • Blood pressure, sugar and eye check-ups
  • Help to follow up with a local doctor
  • Reminders for medicines and appointments

Bridging generations

School programmes that bring grandparents into classrooms to share skills and stories.

  • Grandparents' day in schools and colleges
  • Students record family stories and recipes
  • The yearly school selfie challenge

Rights and dignity

Helping elders understand pension, welfare schemes and protection against neglect.

  • Simple guides to pensions and benefits
  • Help with forms and documents
  • Pointing families to legal and support services

Digital inclusion

Teaching parents to video-call, pay bills and stay connected with family far away.

  • Phone help sessions run by volunteers
  • Spotting online scams and fake calls
  • Video calls with family living far away
